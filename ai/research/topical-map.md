## Context

GhostWorkersCompensationInsurance.com targets a hyper-niche, high-CPC segment: employers, sole proprietors, contractors, and small businesses who need a "ghost policy" workers compensation certificate — a policy issued to an employer with no covered employees that satisfies state certificate-of-insurance requirements for contracting, licensing, or vendor qualification purposes. Core buyer pain points: audit protection, avoiding payroll overstatement penalties, compliance without active employees.

**Core keyword cluster:** "ghost policy workers compensation" = 1,600 monthly searches, CPC $42, HIGH competition. The domain is brand-matched to this term — a massive structural SEO advantage.

## Strategic Takeaways

1. **The ghost policy cluster is brand-matched but narrow.** "Ghost policy workers compensation" and its 4 variants all score 1,600/mo — effectively 6,400 total searches for the exact product. CPC of $42 confirms commercial intent. The domain name is a direct match — this site should own rank 1–3 for all variants within 6–9 months of publishing.

2. **"Workers comp exemption" is the sleeper hit.** 8,100 searches/month, LOW competition (index 9), $18 CPC. Most of these searchers are actually ghost policy candidates. A well-optimized comparison page targeting this term alone could drive more traffic than the entire ghost policy cluster.

3. **Sole proprietor / self-employed funnel is the biggest volume bridge.** Workers comp sole proprietor (2,900/mo), sole proprietor workers comp (320/mo), workers comp for self employed (390/mo) — these are upstream of the ghost policy purchase and represent a large qualified audience.

4. **CPC signals premium monetization.** Top-of-page bids: $61–$124 for ghost policy and small business terms. Even with organic traffic, this validates affiliate/lead gen potential at premium rates.

5. **State pages have outsized opportunity.** NC ghost policy queries carry $100 CPCs. State-specific pages targeting NC, TX, FL, GA, NJ, VA are quick wins with low content competition and high buyer intent.

6. **No direct domain competitors visible in DataForSEO.** The ghost policy SERP returned 0 domain competitors — meaning this is a genuinely unclaimed content niche. First-mover with a complete topical cluster wins.
